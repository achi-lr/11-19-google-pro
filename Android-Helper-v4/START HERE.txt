Android Helper — version 4

1. Extract the entire ZIP. Run START.bat or AndroidHelper.exe.
   Python is bundled. Install MuMu separately.
2. Enable Root and ADB → Enable local connection in MuMu.
3. Click Open Google sign-in and wait for the screen to load in MuMu.
   If Android shows an account provider list, select Google.
   Sign in or create your account manually in MuMu.
4. Open Google One in MuMu. Install Google One opens its Play Store page.
5. Click Check connection and check the selected Play Store account.
   Use the step buttons in order and check the result in MuMu.

EN / RU at the top right switches the interface and future log messages.
Existing log entries keep their original language.
The theme button switches between light and dark.
Both preferences are saved to %LOCALAPPDATA%\AndroidHelper\settings.json.

The app opens Google sign-in. It does not enter credentials or click Next.
After opening sign-in, check the connection again before running a step.
Google's own screen supports signing in and creating an account.

The original subscription functions are retained: the script may confirm
a detected zero price. Watch the price and period in MuMu and check the
next payment date after each step. Open subscriptions opens a separate page.

Logs and reports: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AndroidHelper.
Source code: src/. Rebuild: build.ps1. Integrity check: verify-files.ps1.
Offer availability depends on Google and the selected account.
